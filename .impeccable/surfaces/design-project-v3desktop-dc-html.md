Scope: Fixth marketing landing page, desktop (1440). Mode: Persuade. Redesign of version 1 (design/project/Main.dc.html), whose look is evidence only.

Audience and action: Nigerian engineering undergraduates searching for SIWES/IT/internship placements (primary) and engineering companies hiring them (secondary). Primary action: search placements / create a verified profile. Proof: two-sided verification, the status tracker with read receipts, no-fee promise (confirmed), CAC checks. No invented stats, quotes or logos.

## Direction contract
FORM: Nigerian construction-site project signboard + blue mandatory safety signs (seed:2667fba522122c47, assigned).
THESIS: The page is the signboard planted at the gate of a Nigerian site, announcing a project: your placement. It refuses the dark-glow SaaS hero with a centred headline over feature cards.
OWN-WORLD: Off-white hoarding ground, navy ink, brand blue for board headers and the round mandatory signs, cyan reserved only for the student's own path (status they've reached, next step). Flat colour fields, no gradients or glows. Barlow Condensed bold caps for signboard labels and display; Barlow for text; tabular figures for stipends and durations. Square-cornered boards, thick borders like painted signs, posts as structure.
STORY: Who it's for (the signboard says "Project: your SIWES placement"), why it's trustworthy (mandatory signs: verify, no fees, verified companies only), how it works (work-progress board), what's on offer (listing ticker on hoarding, placement types), companies' path (contractor notice board), close (site gate pass = sign up).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo of young Nigerian women engineers on site; a white project signboard on navy posts stands over its left half, with PROJECT / CLIENT / CONTRACTOR / CONSULTANT / DURATION fields and a working placement search (what, discipline, state) as its bottom field.
SIGNATURE: Mandatory blue-circle signs that state the platform's rules; hovering a sign lifts its plate to reveal the rule in full.
RAISES: from orienteering, one reserved colour (cyan) for the visitor's own path; from seven-segment totems, numbers in fixed tabular slots; from OSPAAAL, flat fearless colour fields; from Coptic binding, the board's structure (fields, rules, posts) shown, not hidden. Challengers declined on audience identification and product clarity.
