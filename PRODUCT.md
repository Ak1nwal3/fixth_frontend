# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** Nigerian engineering undergraduates (about 18–25, mostly 300–400 level, universities and polytechnics) looking for mandatory SIWES placements, industrial training (IT) and internships. They mostly browse on mid-range Android phones on costly data, often under deadline pressure, and want to know a listing is real, fits their discipline, and where their application stands.
- **Secondary:** Nigerian engineering companies and their HR teams (oil and gas, power, construction, manufacturing, steel, telecoms, aviation maintenance) recruiting SIWES students, trainees and interns.
- **Later:** fresh graduates and NYSC corps members (graduate roles are future scope).

## Product Purpose
A single trusted place where verified engineering students discover verified placements from engineering companies and apply through a structured process. Success means verified students, verified companies, published placements, applications and, ultimately, successful placements.

## Positioning
Built only for engineering, and verified on both sides: students pass school-email/ID and live-photo verification; companies are checked against their CAC registration and approved by the Fixth team before any listing is visible. Placements are earned on merit, not through "who you know". Fixth never charges students for a placement (confirmed by the client, 2026-10-22).

## Operating Context
Openings today circulate in WhatsApp groups, notice boards, lecturers' contacts and company websites; scams that charge "processing fees" are common. SIWES is a degree requirement coordinated with ITF; students keep a logbook and need placement and acceptance letters. Academic calendars shift because of strikes and session delays.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Students: verified profile (institution, discipline, level, preferred locations, availability, skills, CV, projects), search and filter by discipline/location/duration, one-step apply with no cover letter, status tracking (Submitted → Viewed → Shortlisted → Accepted/Rejected) with read receipts and notifications.
- Companies: verified profile with CAC number, listings with discipline/level restrictions, duration and stipend, a shareable application link, applicant filters, one-click status updates. Free for companies during launch.
- Coming soon: AI screening with explained 0–100% match scores (humans make every decision), graduate roles, mobile app, messaging.
- Open: student pricing beyond "never charged for a placement" is not specified.

## Brand Commitments
- The lowercase "fixth" wordmark is fixed (blue #0F35DB on light, white on dark).
- The brand palette is fixed: blue #0F35DB / #0C2AAE, navy #00002E, cyan #BCF7FB / #D5FFFF, off-white #F3F3F3, white.
- Voice: Nigerian English, British spelling; warm, direct and credible; local terms (SIWES, IT, ITF, CAC, NYSC, matric number, 400L, HOD, logbook) used without explanation.

## Product Principles
1. Trust before volume: every listing and applicant is verified.
2. Merit over connections.
3. No black holes: students always know where they stand.
4. Light and phone-first for Nigerian networks and devices.
5. Engineering only.
