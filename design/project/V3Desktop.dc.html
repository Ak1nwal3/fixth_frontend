Questions we get

Still unsure? Send us a message on WhatsApp at [+234 XXX XXX XXXX].

Do I need to know somebody to get placed?+

No. Verified companies see your verified profile, your skills and your CV. That’s the whole point.

Will Fixth ever ask me to pay?+

Never for a placement. If anyone asks you for a fee in Fixth’s name, don’t pay, and report it to us.

I don’t have a school email. Can I still join?+

Yes. Use a personal email and submit your student ID and matric number. We verify it manually.

How do you check the companies?+

Each one submits its CAC registration and is reviewed by our team. Nothing they post is visible until they’re approved.

Our session has been shifted. What about my dates?+

Update your availability any time. You’ll only be matched with placements that fit your new dates.

Do I need a cover letter?+

No. Your profile and CV go in one step.
