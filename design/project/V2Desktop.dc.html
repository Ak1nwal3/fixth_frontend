Questions

Is Fixth free for students?

Yes. Companies can also post and recruit for free while Fixth is launching.

How does student verification work?

Confirm your school email, upload your student ID or a portal screenshot, then take a quick live photo. You can browse while we review; applying unlocks once you’re verified.

I don’t have a school email. Can I join?

Yes. Sign up with a personal email and submit your student ID and matric number for manual verification.

How are companies verified?

Each company submits its CAC registration and is reviewed by the Fixth team. Its listings stay hidden until it’s approved.

Do I need a cover letter?

No. Your Fixth profile and uploaded CV are your application, sent in one step.

Which disciplines and places are covered?

Every engineering discipline, with placements across Nigeria. Filter by discipline, state and duration.

When will graduate roles arrive?

Graduate trainee and entry-level roles for fresh graduates and NYSC members are planned after launch. Sign up and we’ll tell you first.
