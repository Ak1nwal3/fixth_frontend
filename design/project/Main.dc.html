FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Contact our team.

Is Fixth free?+

Yes for students. Companies can also post placements and recruit for free during our launch phase.

How does student verification work?+

Confirm your school email, upload your student ID or a portal screenshot, then take a quick live photo. You can browse while we review, and applying unlocks once you’re verified.

I don’t have a school email. Can I still join?+

Yes. Sign up with a personal email and submit your student ID and matric number for manual verification.

How do you verify companies?+

Every company submits its CAC registration and is reviewed by our team. Its listings stay hidden until it’s approved.

Do I need a cover letter?+

No. You apply with your Fixth profile and your uploaded CV in one step.

Which disciplines and locations are covered?+

Every engineering discipline, with placements across Nigeria. Filter by discipline, location and duration to find what fits.
